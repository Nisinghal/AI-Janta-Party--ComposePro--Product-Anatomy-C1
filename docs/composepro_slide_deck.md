# ComposePro — 12-Slide Research & Pitch Deck

## Slide 1 — Title & One-Liner

**ComposePro**

Real-time, visual "expert girlfriend" coach in your camera view

- Shows exactly where to place subject, align horizon, angle to light
- Simple on-screen guides (dots, lines, highlights), not long text
- Built for smartphone-first users who want better photos now

---

## Slide 2 — The Problem

**Beginners know what looks good but can't set it up in the moment**

- Most common mistakes: centered subjects, tilted horizons, messy backgrounds, no look room
- Composition is observational—harder to learn than camera settings
- Passive learning (articles/videos) doesn't help while framing

---

## Slide 3 — Market Context & Timing

**Smartphone photography dominates; users expect smart assistance**

- ~1,440 photos/year per user; 75% from smartphones; 68% shared within 24h
- 52% edit almost every photo; 30% use AI-powered tools
- Live coaching apps already ship (FrameUp, GudoCam, Klick)—proving demand

---

## Slide 4 — Target Users (Personas)

**Four primary segments with distinct needs**

- **Social Sharer (18–34):** better everyday photos with minimal effort
- **Travel & Food Creator (22–40):** consistent aesthetics, faster workflow
- **Enthusiast Upgrader (25–50):** deliberate practice, measurable improvement
- **Local Pro (28–55):** faster setup, fewer reshoots, consistent results

---

## Slide 5 — Needs & Pain Points

**Functional, emotional, and social needs**

- **Functional:** show me where to place subject/horizon; angle to light; keep it visual
- **Emotional:** confidence, control, progress
- **Pains:** observational gap, repeated errors, passive learning, app distrust, battery fears

---

## Slide 6 — Solution (MVP)

**Live overlays + light/angle prompts + progress tracking**

- Rule-of-thirds grid, horizon level, subject placement dot/box
- Light-direction prompts, angle/height nudges, distraction highlights
- Genre presets (Portrait/Landscape/Street/Food/Product)
- Progress: streaks, before/after, simple analytics

---

## Slide 7 — Differentiation

**Own the 'in-the-moment' niche with trust-first design**

- Deterministic overlays + horizon level first, then AI coaching
- Light-direction + angle/height nudges—beyond just grids
- Free core overlays, no watermark, transparent pricing

---

## Slide 8 — User Journeys (Highlights)

**From first win to paid conversion**

- **Social Sharer:** first win <60s; 3-min challenges; Pro at $6.99/mo
- **Travel & Food Creator:** genre presets; consistency score; annual plan
- **Enthusiast:** deliberate-practice loops; analytics; community critique
- **Local Pro:** reshoot-rate reduction; session time saved; annual/lifetime

---

## Slide 9 — Business Model & Benchmarks

**Pricing tests aligned with category medians**

- Tests: $6.99 vs $9.99/month; $39.99–$49.99/year; lifetime for pros
- Benchmarks: Photo & Video ~$12.99/mo, ~$38.42/yr; Education ~$8.38/mo, ~$44.99/yr
- Annual renewal medians: 23–40%

---

## Slide 10 — Go-to-Market

**ASO + short-form content + community challenges**

- Keywords: "composition coach," "rule of thirds camera," "live photography tips"
- 30–60s reels showing before/after with live overlays
- 7-day "One Rule Per Day" challenge with shareable carousels
- Partnerships: micro-influencers (phone photography, travel)

---

## Slide 11 — Feasibility & Plan

**MVP in 4–6 weeks on iOS/Android**

- iOS: AVCapture + Vision (horizon, contours) + Core ML
- Android: CameraX + ML Kit/TFLite (object detection)
- Risks: AI reliability, battery/latency, billing trust
- Mitigations: lite mode, throttled AI, transparent pricing

---

## Slide 12 — Ask & Next Steps

**Validate → Build → Launch**

- Validate: 2-week concierge MVP (30 users, 7-day challenge)
- Build: MVP in 4–6 weeks; beta test with 100 users
- Launch: ASO + short-form + challenges; measure D1/D7/D30, conversion
